# Bigtop Player 0.1.0

The first public build: a cue-based music player for live shows, for Windows.
Download the ZIP, unpack it into a normal folder and start `Bigtop Player.exe`; nothing needs installing.

## What it does

- A step list with one big GO: each GO runs one step. The sound that was playing fades out by itself, or keeps playing, as the step says.
- Fade steps and per-sound changes for running sounds: keep, go to a level, fade out, each with its own time. Every fade has a 10 ms declick, so nothing clicks.
- Several sounds at once, each with a live fader, its own Fade button, Keep (store what you hear) and a position bar you can drag to seek.
- Pause and resume for everything at once, and Fade out (Esc) for everything.
- Three modes, Edit, Rehearsal and Show, so nothing can be changed by accident during a show; what a mode does not allow is hidden.
- A pad grid for effects that play whatever the steps do.
- Art-Net timecode for the lighting desk, with a choice of network adapter.
- Drop WAV, MP3 or FLAC files on the list; they are converted in the background to 48 kHz FLAC inside the show folder.
- Autosave, backups and recovery: after a crash the same show opens at the same step, and nothing plays by itself.
- Export a show to one ZIP and import it on another system.

## Known limits

- Windows only for now; macOS and Linux build from source.
- Some variable-bit-rate MP3 files without a proper header may be imported with their ending cut: listen to each MP3's ending once, or convert it to WAV.
